A personal record of Meili Bai — the places she left behind, the people she met, and the life she continues to build.

CHAPTER 01 / THE BEGINNING

The Lost

Before she knew where she was going.

Meili Bai was born in Hong Kong, where the sea, fishing boats and the sound of machinery became part of the world she knew.

She grew up learning that nothing stays still. People leave. Places change. And sometimes, moving forward is the only choice left.

“Some roads are chosen. Others simply begin beneath your feet.”
CHAPTER 02 / SAR

SAR

Somewhere between losing and finding herself.

The years that followed brought Meili into a different kind of life.

New people. New responsibilities. And experiences that slowly changed the way she looked at the world around her.

CHAPTER 03 / ROUTE 68

Yellow Jack

A place built from dust, gasoline and stubbornness.

Somewhere along Route 68 stood a small bar called Yellow Jack.

It became more than a business. It became a place where people could stop, drink, talk, laugh, and stay for a while.

“Maybe home isn't a place. Maybe it's the people who make you want to stay.”
CHAPTER 04 / LIMITED RP

A New Beginning

New city. New uniform. Same girl.

Eventually, Meili left another chapter behind and found herself starting again.

This time, she chose a different path. Not behind a bar. Not beneath the hood of a car. But beside people who needed help.

She became an EMT.

“She doesn't need saving. She just doesn't always know how to save herself.”
