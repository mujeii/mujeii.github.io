02 / BACKGROUND

Where She Came From

Born in Hong Kong, Meili grew up around the sea, fishing communities and the sound of machinery.

She spent much of her younger years around boats and workshops, eventually learning mechanics from Uncle Bobby's repair shop.

Machines made sense to her. They broke for a reason. They could be taken apart, understood, and sometimes put back together.

People were never quite that simple.

“She learned how to fix machines long before she learned how to fix herself.”
03 / LIFE

The Roads Behind Her

Her life took her through different cities, different people and different versions of herself.

From The Lost, to SAR, and eventually Yellow Jack along Route 68, each chapter left something behind.

Yellow Jack became more than a bar. It was a place she built with her own hands — a place where strangers could become friends and where she could pretend, for a while, that she had finally found somewhere to stay.
